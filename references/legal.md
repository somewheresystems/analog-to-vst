# Legal checklist

Not legal advice. Circuit modeling is common practice (many commercial plugins do it), within these lines:

- **Circuits and measured behavior are facts.** Topologies and component values may be modeled. Patents on classic gear have long expired; check for newer designs.
- **Don't copy files.** Write your own netlist from values. Don't redistribute schematics, service manuals, firmware, impulse responses or captures made by others.
- **Names.** Brand and model names are trademarks: use them only to describe what was modeled ("modeled after the ..."), never as the plugin's name or in a way that suggests endorsement. Add a disclaimer that trademarks belong to their owners.
- **Look.** Don't copy faceplates, logos or trade dress.
- **Digital hardware** (reverbs, delays with firmware): behavior can be measured and re-created; code cannot be copied.
- **Licensing.** The template is MIT. nih-plug's VST3 support uses GPLv3 bindings, so distributed VST3 binaries are GPLv3; CLAP binaries can stay MIT.
